Silo families

Eight standard types, one fabrication line.

Silo design starts with the stored product: particle size, abrasion, moisture, temperature, and discharge method define the shell, hopper, and fittings.

Dry bulkBolted

Bolted steel silos

Panel-built silos assembled from rolled steel sheets with bolted vertical and horizontal joints, delivered flat-packed and erected on site with minimal equipment.

Function: cement, lime, gypsum, and aggregate storage where speed of erection, relocatability, and cost efficiency decide the design.

Food gradeWelded

Stainless steel silos

Fully welded stainless shells with smooth internal surfaces, inspection manholes, and sanitary fittings that keep stored product clean and easy to discharge.

Function: flour, sugar, food ingredients, and process powders where hygiene, corrosion resistance, and clean discharge dominate the brief.

CementBatching plants

Cement silos

Cylindrical silos with steep hopper bottoms, aeration pads, dust vents, and load cells for weight control — sized for batching plant daily consumption.

Function: cement storage for ready-mix and block plants, with pneumatic filling and fast, accurate draw-off into the batch cycle.

GrainAgriculture

Grain and seed silos

High-capacity flat-bottom or hopper-bottom silos with ventilation, aeration floors, and temperature monitoring for safe storage of harvested product.

Function: wheat, barley, corn, and seed storage on farms, ports, and feed mills — protecting quality across long storage seasons.

PowderFly ash

Powder and fly-ash silos

Welded silos with fluidising aeration, dust collection, level probes, and venting designed for fine, abrasive powders that must flow without arching.

Function: fly ash, silica powder, and industrial dust storage with reliable pneumatic fill and discharge for plants and concrete producers.

ConcreteHigh volume

Concrete silo systems

Engineered concrete silo batteries for very high volumes, with steel discharge cones, gallery access, and centralised product handling between cells.

Function: bulk terminals and mills where capacity in the millions of tonnes demands fixed infrastructure rather than relocatable steel.

PortsTerminals

Port and terminal silos

Terminal-scale storage with fast fill and discharge rates, weighbridges, conveyor and elevator integration, and marine-grade coating for coastal sites.

Function: import–export grain and bulk handling where shiploading rates, stock control, and turnaround time set the storage design.

BulkGalvanised

Bulk galvanised steel silos

Galvanised corrugated silos with bolted assembly, weathertight roofs, and aerated floors — the standard choice for distributed farm and depot storage.

Function: grain and feed storage across multiple small sites where low cost, corrosion resistance, and local erection are the priority.
