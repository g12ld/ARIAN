What we do

Capabilities

End-to-end industrial services from concept to completion — backed by ISO-certified processes and decades of field experience.

Structural steel

Design, fabrication, and erection of structural steel frameworks for commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects.

Metal fabrication

Precision sheet metal, plate work, welding, and custom fabrication using CNC laser cutting and bending.

Industrial equipment

Supply, installation, and maintenance of heavy machinery, conveyor systems, and production line equipment.

Piping & pressure vessels

ASME-certified fabrication of piping systems, pressure vessels, storage tanks, and heat exchangers for process industries.

Maintenance & repair

On-site and in-house maintenance, refurbishment, and emergency repair services for industrial plant equipment.

Engineering consulting

Feasibility studies, structural analysis, value engineering, and project management from concept through commissioning.
